System Overview

Store Inventory and Sales Management System

The Store Inventory and Sales Management System is a web-based application designed to help small neighborhood convenience stores manage their daily business operations. It provides features for managing products, monitoring inventory, recording sales transactions, managing customers and suppliers, and generating reports. The system aims to reduce manual work, improve record accuracy, and make store management more organized and efficient.

Product Records

Organize product names, categories, and prices.

Stock Monitoring

Check quantities and identify items that need restocking.

Sales Tracking

Record transactions and automatically update stock.

General Objective

To develop a web-based Store Inventory and Sales Management System that helps small neighborhood convenience stores manage products, monitor inventory, record sales transactions, and generate reports efficiently, accurately, and conveniently.

Specific Objectives

The system is designed to support these everyday store operations:

Product Management

Provide a platform for adding, updating, deleting, searching, and viewing product information.

Inventory Management

Monitor product quantities, track stock movements, and maintain accurate inventory records.

Sales Management

Record sales transactions, calculate purchase totals, and update inventory after every sale.

Low-Stock Monitoring

Identify products with low stock levels and help store owners determine what needs restocking.

Customer and Supplier Management

Organize and maintain customer and supplier information.

Report Generation

Generate sales and inventory reports to help store owners monitor business performance.

User Management

Provide separate administrator and staff access with appropriate permissions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a simple, reliable, and user-friendly inventory and sales management solution that helps small convenience stores organize their daily operations, improve record accuracy, reduce manual work, and manage their business more effectively through technology.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a practical and accessible digital management solution for small neighborhood stores, helping business owners improve their productivity, make better decisions, and adapt to modern technology in managing their businesses.

Ethical Standards and Principles

Principles that guide responsible handling of accounts, information, and store records.

Security

The system ensures that only authorized users can access specific features and information. It protects user accounts and business records through proper authentication and security practices.

Implementation in Our System

  • Password hashing to protect user passwords.
  • Login authentication and session management.
  • Role-based access control for administrators and staff.
  • Prepared statements to help prevent SQL injection.

Privacy and Data Protection

The system respects the privacy of customers, staff, and administrators by protecting their personal information and limiting access to authorized users.

Implementation in Our System

  • Collect only necessary personal information.
  • Restrict access to sensitive information.
  • Prevent unauthorized disclosure of customer and employee records.
  • Handle stored information responsibly.

Data Accuracy and Integrity

The system maintains accurate, consistent, and reliable records of products, inventory, and sales transactions.

Implementation in Our System

  • Automatically calculate sales totals.
  • Deduct inventory quantities after successful transactions.
  • Validate product prices and quantities.
  • Prevent negative stock quantities.
  • Maintain consistent sales and inventory records.
